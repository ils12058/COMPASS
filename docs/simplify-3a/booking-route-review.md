# Route review: Student appointment booking

Baseline `4bfc1b4185c46cff2a40598f071bad1f2e40168e`. Route `/portal/appointments/book` ([entry](../../fe/src/app/(portal)/portal/appointments/book/page.tsx)) uses the portal shell and `pageSheetWidth`. The default route composes `AppointmentBookingPage`, `AppointmentsLocalNavigation`, `PageHeader`, `StudentBookingPathways`, `IllustratedIntroduction`, `Panel`/`PanelSection`, standard form controls and `CanonicalPagination`. The `pathway=FIXED_DATE_SLOT` query selects a separate scheduled-slot workspace; `service_id`, `search` and `page` carry context. Group discovery is a separate route, not a query variant of the booking form.

**Access from source:** the main booking workspace requires the Student role, current lifecycle status and `appointments.manage_self`. Other accounts receive the unavailable state. Fixed-slot browsing additionally requires `appointments.view_self` and `services.catalog.view`; group browsing requires the Student role and `appointments.view_self`. Those navigation checks do not establish that a slot or session exists or that the backend will authorize a particular action.

**Primary tasks and states:** choose an individual service, delivery mode, counselor and freshly checked time, then review and book; or navigate to published fixed slots or group discovery. Relevant variants include permission/lifecycle denial; service loading, error, empty, search and pagination; selection and prerequisite; counselor and slot loading, empty and error; slot freshness and conflict; success and uncertain booking outcomes. Fixed slots and group registration have distinct access, availability, conflict and reconciliation states, requiring their own route review.

**Evidence and disposition:** the [existing synthetic desktop render](../simplify-2c/screenshots/after-booking-1440.png) shows the three-row pathway panel above service selection (S3-001). [Draft PR #343](https://github.com/ils12058/COMPASS/pull/343) proposes the compact composition and documents new synthetic screenshots and interaction checks at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px. It is a proposed fix, not an integration-baseline closure. Revalidate the final merged version, its complete role/state matrix and outstanding accessibility/UAT coverage before closing this route.
