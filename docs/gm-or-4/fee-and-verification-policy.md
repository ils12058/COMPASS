# Fee and physical-inspection evidence

PHP 80.00 for each F4/F6 certificate is **team-reported practice**, not a signed institutional schedule. No real effective date, waiver, excess payment treatment, refund rule or charging reference date is supplied. Operational `resolve_approved_fee` still returns `None`; ACTIVE without an applicable approved decision refuses inspection, preparation and new issuance. Neither an environment variable, a client flag, an API field, IT override nor Head designation supplies that approval.

Synthetic tests inject `SYNTHETIC-ONLY` / `fixture-v1`, PHP `Decimal("80.00")`, explicitly fictional authority `isolated-test-approval`, receipt-date applicability bounded from 2000-01-01 through 2099-12-31. These dates deliberately belong only to the fixture. They establish no institutional historical boundary. Both variants, zero/75/100 mismatches, missing/mismatched/changed decisions, invalid or unknown applicability, amount precision and policy loss are exercised. Entered actual amount is preserved. Recorded decision identity/version/evidence remains on the immutable inspection and is referenced by issuance.

Services compare policy snapshots during inspection/preparation/issuance. A pending changed decision requires deliberate reinspection/re-preparation; it does not rewrite an older attestation. Already issued historical downloads and recorded evidence survive unavailable/new current policy. Multi-process policy replacement requires coordinated writer drain; test-injected policy changes are transaction fault tests, not evidence of a live distributed policy registry.

`PHYSICAL_OR_INSPECTION` records a named eligible operator's assertion that they personally inspected the presented physical receipt against the saved applicant/request/number/date/amount. The server owns actor/time. Saving facts never inspects; import/preparation/issuance do not manufacture historical inspection. Receipt changes invalidate the exact old attestation and retain it. Unrelated corrections keep inspection and require preparation again. Cancellation retains reservations and history. Final issuance references the exact claim and inspection reviewed.

A simulated checkbox action is not an actual physical receipt inspection. Physical appearance alone does not prove authenticity or cleared funds. COMPASS does not independently reconcile UCN Cashier payment records. Institution-approved training, accountability and fee authority remain activation blockers.
