# Notices, invalidation and Audit

Existing CONT-2A actions remain the request/approval/decline/cancel/revoke evidence. New registered actions are `counseling.continuity.selection_read` and `counseling.handoff.draft_created/draft_updated/submitted/read`. The existing synchronous Audit service records actor, resource UUID, Grant/Student/author/recipient UUIDs, lifecycle/version and opaque policy/evidence reference. Source scores, narratives, Handoff text, ciphertext, keys, credentials and raw payloads are absent. Denied guessed sources do not produce successful disclosure events.

Body reads, draft writes and submission must commit required Audit in the same durable transaction; final time/policy checks follow Audit insertion. Failure rolls back the body release or write. Identical current-authorized submission replay does not emit a second submission event. Periodic protected rechecks are separate actual authorized reads and each is audited; they are not proof that the person understood/read the text. Monitoring/volume/retention still need institutional review. The existing curated Audit UI is not broadened into a professional-body browser.

**No continuity/Handoff Notification events or delivery producers are activated.** Existing Notification policy/catalog, currentness, generic deep links and content-free realtime architecture were inspected. Concrete recipients, safe copy, delivery currentness and Student transparency remain unapproved. The only accepted new workflow notification/acknowledgment rules are DEFERRED. There are no in-app/email/Push notices, scheduled reminders or acknowledgment endpoint. No pending Handoff notification can therefore be delivered after revoke. Tests verify positive Handoff operations create no Notification rows. The local tab invalidation hint carries only Grant UUID and is not a Notification or approval.

Future approved events may include requested/approved/declined/revoked and Handoff submitted/acknowledged, with exact recipient/currentness checks before retries/delivery and authenticated destination reauthorization. No channel may contain Student concerns, scores, recommendations, text, source excerpts, diagnoses, media or credentials. New Notice/Push/email work must be separately approved; infrastructure availability does not establish authority.

A future approved Student notice may explain temporary guidance coverage, approved receiver identity/dates and a contact path, explicitly distinguishing Appointment reassignment. Whether notice/consent/another basis is needed, its timing, wording, retention and recipients are unresolved. No consent form, Student Handoff access, professional disclosure or delivery is fabricated here.
