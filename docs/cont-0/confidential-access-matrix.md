# Actual confidential access matrix

Current API/service authorization at the verified integration SHA. **Receiving Counselor authorization is not implemented.** A label such as current Appointment Counselor, Head or College-responsible Counselor does not imply all other relationships. Tables evaluate the record in the column, not everything authored by the actor.

Legend: **A** currently allowed with the row's effective capability/ordinary resource predicate; **C** conditionally allowed by the policy table below; **D** currently denied; **F** proposed future explicit selected read, currently denied on that basis. Every A/C also requires supported active actor identity. `O` in a condition means original source assignment; `K` means a qualifying live Context; `S` means self; `R` means current operational College scope. A/C is a source conclusion, not live deployment verification. Institutional decisions are marked **I** in the future-policy section.

## Cross-domain gaps and future classification

Confirmed mismatch: K → cross-author published Summary bodies. Intentional, tested difference: K → submitted full Inventory/Support even where generic R scope denies. Metadata listings of other providers' Appointment/Referral/Call Slip/Routine history are implemented Context behavior; they are not confidential-source access grants. Follow-Up source links deliberately do not bypass source authorization. Assessment oversight is domain-specific and cannot be generalized into Counseling oversight.

**F** for continuity means a new domain-owned selected read adapter and approved institutional policy are needed. **I** includes Student notice/basis, acting delegation, source categories, Inventory/Intake scope and maximum duration. The initial source plan excludes assessment results, message bodies, recordings/transcripts, credentials, Student Summary cross-author access and unsubmitted drafts. See [source policy](source-record-policy.md).

No row asserts that previously viewed content can be recalled. Session/cache clearing and no-store headers reduce persistence but do not erase screenshots, memory, browser artifacts or separately downloaded material.
