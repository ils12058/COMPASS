# Proposed continuity source disclosure policy

All new permissions here are **proposed**, gated by GCO/privacy approval and CONT-2B domain adapters. Current access remains the [actual matrix](confidential-access-matrix.md). “Allowed” means eligible for the proposed grant design, never a currently implemented entitlement.

## Scope comparison

Category grants are compact but can silently include future records or sensitive fields added later. Explicit UUID selection is inspectable but needs source validation and cannot make a forbidden record disclosable. Use a hybrid: a fixed, minimal operational context for an approved Student/receiver plus **explicit record-ID selections for every protected source**. Do not automatically include new records created after approval. No `ALL_HISTORY`, arbitrary ORM type, wildcard College, dynamic filter or generic “view all records” action.

Initial CONT-2B should start with selected Encounter structural overview and finalized Routine Evaluation, plus only the narrow Referral/Follow-Up projections institutionally approved. Do not implement every possible adapter before its actual need is agreed. Every allowed kind must have both an approval-time selector policy and a read-time disclosure adapter; missing adapter means denial.

## Selection when original Counselor is absent

A leader needs source **metadata** to propose exact UUIDs without gaining content access. Define a focused continuity selection endpoint authorized for eligible request/approval actors: Student identity plus resource kind/UUID/original author/date/finalized/eligible flag only, scoped to the coverage request. Exclude scores, Intake concerns, reason text, titles derived from narratives, media, credentials and unrelated third-party identity. Cap/paginate before returning metadata, audit enumeration, and avoid global Student search beyond the actor's approved organizational scope.

Metadata enumeration itself is new disclosure and requires approval of that scope. Existing Head oversight of Referrals/Inventory does not imply Routine enumeration. If GCO declines metadata selection by a leader, the unplanned workflow needs another authorized records custodian procedure; do not fall back to all-history access. A receiving Counselor cannot add arbitrary guessed UUIDs to an approved grant.

## Version, integrity and residual restrictions

Approval covers the reviewed source/projection. Some source domains have `updated_at` rather than an explicit resource version. Persist a structural revision marker at approval; a changed source is denied pending refresh/reapproval unless an institutionally approved lifecycle-specific rule proves the same immutable content. Do not hash/log decrypted content merely to detect change. Source deletion/void/reopen/disposition, incorrect Student, wrong historical author link, corruption and policy revision can each deny a grant-backed read.

No copied clinical store or denormalized encrypted “all Student history” snapshot. Optional Handoff narrative is a distinct authored communication, not a generated duplicate Evaluation/diagnosis. Its content must remain minimum necessary and cannot be used to launder excluded media/assessment/message content into scope. Professional judgment and privacy review govern necessary human-authored information; this system does not infer diagnoses or clinical recommendations.

Source-level authorization remains independent of College changes: a grant does not change historical authorship or current responsibility. Recommended transfer handling is deny new grant reads until organizational scope is revalidated, without rewriting original grant evidence. Whether to require revocation/reapproval on every College transfer is institutional decision D10.
