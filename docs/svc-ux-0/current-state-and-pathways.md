**VERIFIED:** active Service, configured pathway, published offering and eligibility are distinct. `appointment_booking_enabled` is the flexible switch, not a global gate. Modes limit new work; Counselor coverage is all or selected active Counselors, independent of group facilitator assignments and College default routing. An active Service must meet activation conditions, which vary by configuration. See [Catalog services](../../be/compass/service_catalog/services.py#L389) and [editor](../../fe/src/features/services/service-editor-page.tsx#L542).

**VERIFIED:** `requires_current_inventory`, duration and cancellation cutoff are constrained to the flexible-enabled Service configuration; switching flexible off clears them. Fixed booking calls the same Inventory validator when the flag is set, while Group registration does not. A fixed-only Service thus has no configurable current-Inventory flag under this model. Fixed slot times use their own interval; the created Appointment snapshots the Service cutoff. Do not present the settings as independently configurable for fixed-only Services. [Model constraint](../../be/compass/service_catalog/models.py#L82), [Catalog tests](../../be/tests/test_service_catalog.py#L215), [fixed booking](../../be/compass/service_catalog/fixed_slots_services.py#L619).

**VERIFIED:** conflict checks use half-open intervals and shared user locks across Appointments, available fixed slots, published group facilitators and registered Students. Cancelled/non-reserving history does not block; adjacency is allowed. See [scheduling](../../be/compass/service_catalog/scheduling.py#L30) and CORR-1 section of ADR-SVC-1. Existing Appointment snapshots and Group attendance/registration history must not be rewritten by configuration edits. [Consequence review](../../be/compass/service_catalog/services.py#L568).
