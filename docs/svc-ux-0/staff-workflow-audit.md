# Staff workflow audit

**VERIFIED:** Service administrators enter Services, search/filter, create an inactive Service, edit identity, modes, flexible booking settings, fixed/group flags, and provider coverage, then activate separately. The list reports active status, mode and appointment booking but does not summarize the other pathways ([list](../../fe/src/features/services/services-list-page.tsx#L174)). Editor's “Appointment booking” precedes “Additional booking pathways” and describes the latter as “future” even though both are implemented ([editor](../../fe/src/features/services/service-editor-page.tsx#L418)). The detail already explains independent flags and separate group facilitators ([detail](../../fe/src/features/services/service-detail-page.tsx#L324)).

Changing scheduling-sensitive configuration can require a consequence review and explicit acknowledgement; the transaction preserves existing commitments rather than silently cancelling or rewriting them ([backend](../../be/compass/service_catalog/services.py#L568), [editor](../../fe/src/features/services/service-editor-page.tsx#L807)). Counseling copy separates online delivery, new appointments, E-Counseling readiness and existing saved appointments ([shared copy](../../fe/src/features/services/services-shared.tsx#L123), [help](../../fe/src/features/services/service-help.tsx#L6)). Keep these distinctions.

## Operational management and access gap

**VERIFIED:** Fixed slot management component lists slots, obtains scoped operational provider candidates, publishes, updates and withdraws ([panel](../../fe/src/features/services/service-fixed-slots-panel.tsx#L37)). Its only call site is Service detail ([detail](../../fe/src/features/services/service-detail-page.tsx#L385)); all `/portal/services/*` routes pass [ServicesGate](../../fe/src/features/services/services-shared.tsx#L237), requiring *both* catalog view and Service management ([access](../../fe/src/features/services/services-access.ts#L8)). A default Counselor or GSS has appointment management and catalog view but not Service management ([grants](../../be/compass/accounts/policy.py#L605)). Backend slot authority is Counselor self or directly supervised GSS's Counselor, subject to active account and capability ([scope](../../be/compass/service_catalog/access.py#L18)); therefore ordinary authorized operators can be blocked by the enclosing frontend gate. A Head Counselor may satisfy both; an IT Admin can manage configuration but is not automatically slot staff. Source-level finding; the component-only regression at [test](../../fe/tests/service-fixed-slots.test.mjs) does not prove the layout is reachable.

**VERIFIED:** Group Sessions has a separate operational workspace and service picker, accessible without Service management, and an embedded panel for authorized staff on Service detail ([staff page](../../fe/src/features/group-sessions/group-sessions-staff-page.tsx), [picker](../../fe/src/features/services/service-group-sessions-panel.tsx#L1501)). Organizer, current direct supervision, and active facilitator determine per-session authority; a facilitator's participant access does not automatically imply event lifecycle management or roster export ([access](../../be/compass/group_sessions/access.py#L100)). Delivery, publication, attendance, capacity and conflict rules remain server-owned. Disabling a Service or pathway does not erase published records.

**PROPOSED:** show three coequal configuration descriptions with explicit “configured” vs “published/eligible” language; link to existing scoped operational destinations where allowed. Add an appointment-scoped fixed-slot entry that reuses existing panel/APIs and honors per-provider authority and MFA. Keep catalog management distinct; do not grant Service management to reach a slot. Verify any read-only management context independently instead of broadening this gate.
