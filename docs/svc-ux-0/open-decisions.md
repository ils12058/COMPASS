# Open decisions

- **APPROVAL REQUIRED — GCO and team:** Which Student-facing wording best explains flexible individual appointments, published individual slots and group events? Validate with users rather than treating these audit labels as approved institutional copy.
- **APPROVAL REQUIRED — GCO / institutional policy:** Should current Individual Inventory be required for a fixed-only Service, and if so under what policy? Current storage ties the flag to flexible enablement while fixed booking checks it when present. A change would be a backend policy/contract decision, not a UI-only switch. Should Group Sessions ever have a prerequisite? No current group Inventory check was found.
- **APPROVAL REQUIRED — product/team:** Should the Student discovery experience begin with a service overview, remain task-first with stronger contextual links, or support both? Test against multiple pathways, sparse offerings and expected catalog size; do not assume a universal wizard.
- **APPROVAL REQUIRED — team:** Is a pathway-filtered catalog/read projection needed for pagination, opportunity counts or service context? Existing `servicesList` filters flexible booking but not other pathway codes ([API](../../be/compass/service_catalog/api.py#L403)); local page filtering can yield empty pages. Any projection must avoid leaking scope or claiming availability from configuration.
- **APPROVAL REQUIRED — GCO and team:** Where should scoped fixed-slot management live for Counselor and GSS, and which historical slot states should appear? Preserve provider/supervision authorization and actual MFA requirement; do not grant Service management for convenience.
- **APPROVAL REQUIRED — team:** Define response-loss recovery for fixed bookings and group registrations with existing authorized reads. Test ambiguous success before deciding whether a narrowly scoped backend contract is needed; never imply the flexible replay mechanism exists elsewhere.
- **APPROVAL REQUIRED — GCO:** Confirm how published changes, cancellations and delivery details should be explained and whether current notifications and history meet operational expectations. Existing delivery policies, email privacy and E-Counseling readiness are independently governed.
- **OPEN VALIDATION:** Screen-reader, native zoom, user research, real authenticated integration, institutional UAT and deployment status are not established by this audit. See [validation](validation.md).
